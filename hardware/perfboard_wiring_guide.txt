================================================================================
FALL SENSOR SYSTEM - PERFBOARD / PROTOTYPE WIRING DIRECTORY
================================================================================
Based on KiCad Carrier PCB: fall_sensor.kicad_pcb
Total Connected Nets: 21 | Total Components: 21

This guide provides a functional, point-to-point connection list for assembling 
the Fall Sensor system on a prototype perfboard or breadboard before finalizing 
the printed circuit board.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 1: COMPONENT & MODULE PINOUT REFERENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. U1: ESP32 DevKit V1 (30-Pin NodeMCU ESP-WROOM-32)
   - Left Row (Pins 1-15):
     Pin 1:  3V3              Pin 9:  GPIO25 (PRESENCE_PIN)
     Pin 2:  EN (NC)          Pin 10: GPIO26 (FALL_PIN)
     Pin 3:  GPIO36/VP (NC)   Pin 11: GPIO27 (RADAR_EN)
     Pin 4:  GPIO39/VN (NC)   Pin 12: GPIO14 (NC)
     Pin 5:  GPIO34 (BATT_ADC)Pin 13: GPIO12 (NC)
     Pin 6:  GPIO35 (NC)      Pin 14: GND
     Pin 7:  GPIO32 (NC)      Pin 15: GPIO13 (PIR_WAKE)
     Pin 8:  GPIO33 (SYNC_BTN)

   - Right Row (Pins 16-30):
     Pin 16: VIN (NC)         Pin 24: GPIO18 (NC)
     Pin 17: GND              Pin 25: GPIO5 (NC)
     Pin 18: GPIO23 (NC)      Pin 26: GPIO17 (RADAR_TX)
     Pin 19: GPIO22 (NC)      Pin 27: GPIO16 (RADAR_RX)
     Pin 20: TX0 (NC)         Pin 28: GPIO4 (MIC_WAKE)
     Pin 21: RX0 (NC)         Pin 29: GPIO2 (NC)
     Pin 22: GPIO21 (NC)      Pin 30: GPIO15 (NC)
     Pin 23: GPIO19 (FALL_LED)

2. U2: Digispark ATtiny85 (Low-Power Sensor Co-Processor)
   - Pin 1: P5 / RST (NC)
   - Pin 2: P4 (MIC_SIG Input from LM393 DO)
   - Pin 3: P3 (NC)
   - Pin 4: P2 (PIR_WAKE Output to ESP32 GPIO13)
   - Pin 5: P1 (PIR_SIG Input from AM312 OUT)
   - Pin 6: P0 (MIC_WAKE Output to ESP32 GPIO4)
   - Pin 7: GND
   - Pin 8 / 9: VIN / 5V (Connected to +3.3V Logic Rail)

3. U3: MT3608 Step-Up Boost Converter Module
   - Pin 1: VIN+ (Battery switched input via SW2)
   - Pin 2: VIN- / GND
   - Pin 3: VOUT+ (Boosted +5.0V output, trimpot-adjusted)
   - Pin 4: GND

4. U4: TP4056 LiPo Battery Charging & Protection Module
   - Pin 1: BAT+ (Direct connection to BT1 Pad 2)
   - Pin 2: BAT- (Direct connection to BT1 Pad 1)
   - Pin 3: OUT+ (Protected battery output to SW2, C1, and R1)
   - Pin 4: OUT- / GND (Common system ground)

5. U5: LM393 Sound Detection Sensor Module
   - Pin 1: VCC (+3.3V rail)
   - Pin 2: GND
   - Pin 3: DO (Digital Output to ATtiny85 P4)
   - Pin 4: AO (Analog Output, NC)

6. U6: Load Switch (SOT-23-6 DBVT or discrete P-MOSFET circuit)
   - Pin 1: VIN (+5V boosted rail)
   - Pins 2, 4, 5: GND
   - Pin 3: EN (Enable control from ESP32 GPIO27)
   - Pin 6: VOUT (Switched +5V power to Radar VIN)

7. U7: LM2596 Step-Down Buck Converter Module
   - Pin 1: IN+ (+5V from MT3608 VOUT+)
   - Pin 2: IN- (GND)
   - Pin 3: OUT+ (+3.3V high-current logic rail, trimpot-adjusted)
   - Pin 4: OUT- (GND)

8. RADAR1: C1001 60GHz mmWave Fall Detection Radar
   - Pin 1: VIN (Switched +5V from U6 Pin 6)
   - Pin 2: GND
   - Pin 3: TX (UART TX to ESP32 GPIO17/RX)
   - Pin 4: RX (UART RX to ESP32 GPIO16/TX)
   - Pin 5: OUT1 (Digital Presence Detection to ESP32 GPIO25)
   - Pin 6: OUT2 (Digital Fall Alert to ESP32 GPIO26)

9. PIR1: AM312 Mini PIR Motion Sensor
   - Pin 1: VCC (+3.3V rail)
   - Pin 2: OUT (Digital Motion signal to ATtiny85 P1)
   - Pin 3: GND

10. Switches, Battery & UI Components:
   - BT1: 18650 Battery Solder Pads (Pin 1: BAT-, Pin 2: BAT+)
   - SW1: Tactile Pushbutton (SYNC button)
   - SW2: SPST Power Toggle Switch (Main System Power)
   - D1:  Status LED (Pin 1: Anode A, Pin 2: Cathode K)
   - R1:  100kΩ Resistor (Battery Divider High-Side)
   - R2:  100kΩ Resistor (Battery Divider Low-Side)
   - R3:  270Ω Resistor (LED Current Limiter)
   - C1:  10µF Capacitor (Battery rail buffer)
   - C3:  470µF 16V Electrolytic Capacitor (Radar +5V pulse buffer)
   - C4:  22µF Capacitor (+5V filter)
   - C7:  0.1µF Ceramic Capacitor (+3.3V decoupling)
   - C8:  0.1µF Ceramic Capacitor (+3.3V decoupling)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2: FUNCTIONAL WIRING NETLIST (GROUP BY GROUP)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

================================================================================
GROUP 1: COMMON GROUND BUS (GND) - Connect all 21 points together
================================================================================
Create a sturdy bare-copper ground rail along the perfboard. Solder each point:
  1.  U4 TP4056       Pin 4  (OUT-)
  2.  U3 MT3608       Pin 2  (VIN-)
  3.  U3 MT3608       Pin 4  (GND)
  4.  U7 LM2596       Pin 2  (IN-)
  5.  U7 LM2596       Pin 4  (OUT-)
  6.  U1 ESP32        Pin 14 (GND)
  7.  U1 ESP32        Pin 17 (GND)
  8.  U2 ATtiny85     Pin 7  (GND)
  9.  PIR1 AM312      Pin 3  (GND)
  10. U5 LM393        Pin 2  (GND)
  11. RADAR1 C1001    Pin 2  (GND)
  12. U6 LoadSwitch   Pin 2  (GND)
  13. U6 LoadSwitch   Pin 4  (GND)
  14. U6 LoadSwitch   Pin 5  (GND)
  15. D1 Status LED   Pin 2  (Cathode K, short leg / flat side)
  16. R2 Resistor     Pin 2  (Low side of 100kΩ divider)
  17. C1 Capacitor    Pin 2  (10µF negative)
  18. C3 Capacitor    Pin 2  (470µF electrolytic negative stripe)
  19. C4 Capacitor    Pin 2  (22µF negative)
  20. C7 Capacitor    Pin 2  (0.1µF ceramic ground)
  21. C8 Capacitor    Pin 2  (0.1µF ceramic ground)

================================================================================
GROUP 2: BATTERY & CHARGING PATH
================================================================================
  1. BT1 Battery Lead (+) [Pin 2] ---------> U4 TP4056 BAT+ [Pin 1]
  2. BT1 Battery Lead (-) [Pin 1] ---------> U4 TP4056 BAT- [Pin 2]
     (Keep these two leads thick and direct to ensure low ESR during charging)

================================================================================
GROUP 3: PROTECTED BATTERY OUTPUT & MAIN POWER SWITCH
================================================================================
  All these 4 points connect together:
  - U4 TP4056 OUT+ [Pin 3]
  - C1 Capacitor (10µF) Positive [Pin 1]
  - R1 Resistor (100kΩ) [Pin 1]
  - SW2 Power Switch Terminal 1 [Pin 1]

  Then from switch:
  - SW2 Power Switch Terminal 2 [Pin 2] ---> U3 MT3608 VIN+ [Pin 1]
  (Closing SW2 connects battery power into the boost converter)

================================================================================
GROUP 4: BOOSTED +5V POWER RAIL (Adjust MT3608 to 5.00V before connecting load)
================================================================================
  All these 5 points connect together:
  - U3 MT3608 VOUT+ [Pin 3]
  - U7 LM2596 IN+ [Pin 1]
  - U6 LoadSwitch VIN [Pin 1]
  - C3 Capacitor (470µF 16V) Positive [Pin 1]
  - C4 Capacitor (22µF) Positive [Pin 1]
  (C3 absorbs current transients when the mmWave radar sweeps)

================================================================================
GROUP 5: REGULATED +3.3V LOGIC RAIL (Adjust LM2596 to 3.30V before connecting)
================================================================================
  All these 8 points connect together:
  - U7 LM2596 OUT+ [Pin 3]
  - U1 ESP32 3V3 [Pin 1]
  - U2 ATtiny85 VCC/VIN [Pin 9 / Pin 8]
  - PIR1 AM312 VCC [Pin 1]
  - U5 LM393 Sound VCC [Pin 1]
  - SW1 SYNC Pushbutton Terminal 2 [Pin 2]
  - C7 Capacitor (0.1µF) [Pin 1]
  - C8 Capacitor (0.1µF) [Pin 1]

================================================================================
GROUP 6: BATTERY VOLTAGE MONITORING DIVIDER (BATT_ADC)
================================================================================
  Connect these 3 points together:
  - R1 Resistor (100kΩ) Pin 2
  - R2 Resistor (100kΩ) Pin 1
  - U1 ESP32 GPIO34 [Pin 5]
  (R1 is tied to battery OUT+, R2 is tied to GND; mid-point gives V_batt / 2)

================================================================================
GROUP 7: SENSOR WAKEUP & CO-PROCESSOR SIGNALS
================================================================================
  1. PIR Signal:
     - PIR1 AM312 OUT [Pin 2] -------------> U2 ATtiny85 Pin 5 (P1)

  2. Acoustic / Sound Signal:
     - U5 LM393 Sound DO [Pin 3] ----------> U2 ATtiny85 Pin 2 (P4)

  3. Co-Processor Wakeup Lines to ESP32:
     - U2 ATtiny85 Pin 4 (P2) -------------> U1 ESP32 GPIO13 [Pin 15] (PIR_WAKE)
     - U2 ATtiny85 Pin 6 (P0) -------------> U1 ESP32 GPIO4  [Pin 28] (MIC_WAKE)

================================================================================
GROUP 8: C1001 mmWAVE RADAR INTERFACE & POWER GATING
================================================================================
  1. Radar Power Switch:
     - U1 ESP32 GPIO27 [Pin 11] -----------> U6 LoadSwitch EN [Pin 3]
     - U6 LoadSwitch VOUT [Pin 6] ---------> RADAR1 C1001 VIN [Pin 1]
     (ESP32 drives GPIO27 HIGH to energize the radar module)

  2. Radar UART Communications:
     - RADAR1 C1001 TX [Pin 3] ------------> U1 ESP32 GPIO17 [Pin 26] (RX2)
     - RADAR1 C1001 RX [Pin 4] ------------> U1 ESP32 GPIO16 [Pin 27] (TX2)

  3. Radar Real-Time Hardware Alert Lines:
     - RADAR1 C1001 OUT1 [Pin 5] ----------> U1 ESP32 GPIO25 [Pin 9]  (PRESENCE)
     - RADAR1 C1001 OUT2 [Pin 6] ----------> U1 ESP32 GPIO26 [Pin 10] (FALL)

================================================================================
GROUP 9: STATUS INDICATORS & USER CONTROLS
================================================================================
  1. Status LED:
     - U1 ESP32 GPIO19 [Pin 23] -----------> R3 Resistor (270Ω) Pin 1
     - R3 Resistor (270Ω) Pin 2 -----------> D1 Status LED Pin 1 (Anode A)
     (D1 Cathode connects to common Ground)

  2. SYNC Pushbutton:
     - SW1 SYNC Button Pin 1 --------------> U1 ESP32 GPIO33 [Pin 8]
     (SW1 Pin 2 is connected to +3.3V rail. Pressing button pulls GPIO33 HIGH)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 3: PERFBOARD STEP-BY-STEP BRING-UP CHECKLIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: POWER SUPPLY CALIBRATION (CRITICAL - DO NOT SKIP)
  1. Wire ONLY the battery path, SW2, MT3608, and LM2596. DO NOT connect ESP32, 
     ATtiny85, or any sensors yet!
  2. Insert battery and toggle SW2 ON.
  3. Measure MT3608 VOUT+ with a digital multimeter:
     - Adjust the tiny brass screw on the MT3608 trimpot until output is EXACTLY 5.00V.
  4. Measure LM2596 OUT+ with a digital multimeter:
     - Adjust the trimpot on the LM2596 until output is EXACTLY 3.30V.
  5. Toggle SW2 OFF.

STEP 2: GROUND & RAILS CONTINUITY CHECK
  1. With power OFF, test continuity with multimeter beeper across all GND points.
  2. Verify NO short circuit between GND and +5V rail (resistance should be > 10kΩ).
  3. Verify NO short circuit between GND and +3.3V rail (resistance should be > 10kΩ).
  4. Verify NO short circuit between +5V and +3.3V rails.

STEP 3: POPULATE MCUs & SENSORS
  1. Solder or plug in U1 ESP32 and U2 ATtiny85 into socket headers.
  2. Plug in PIR1 and LM393 modules.
  3. Power on and verify 3.3V is present on ESP32 3V3 pin and ATtiny85 VCC pin.
  4. Test battery voltage divider at ESP32 GPIO34 (should read approx. 1.8V-2.1V).

STEP 4: RADAR MODULE INTEGRATION
  1. Connect C1001 Radar header.
  2. When ESP32 boots and pulls GPIO27 HIGH, verify 5.0V appears on Radar VIN pin.
  3. Verify UART communication and falling edge detection signals.
